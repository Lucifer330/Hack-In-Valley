About the hackathon

More than a traditional hackathon.

Hack in Valley is an opportunity for ambitious individuals and teams to transform their ideas into meaningful technology solutions. The event is planned as a multi-stage experience, beginning with registrations and team participation, followed by shortlisting and development rounds, and culminating in an offline grand finale in Himachal Pradesh.

  • Work on real-world problem statements.
  • Build functional prototypes and innovative products.
  • Learn from experienced mentors and industry professionals.
  • Connect with developers, founders, startups and technology communities.
  • Present solutions before an expert judging panel.
  • Compete for prizes, recognition and further opportunities.
